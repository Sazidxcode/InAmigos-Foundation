About Us

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation was founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO). It is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization, licensed by the Central Government, with its base in Chhattisgarh and operations that extend across India.

The foundation holds 80G & 12A certifications, is CSR‑1 registered for corporate partnerships, is registered with NITI Aayog, and holds IAF ISO 9001:2015 certification — together reflecting its commitment to transparency, accountability and consistent quality of operations.
